Welcome! I am a prospective law student with a former MSc in Astronomy from Leiden University, South Holland, Netherlands and BSc in Physics (Astrophysics) from the University of California, Santa Cruz. 

My research interests, now hobbies, were in the atmospheres of exoplanets. I had a short stint investigating the theoretical characteristics of hot Jupiter interiors. Additionally, I was briefly a planet hunter. Nowadays, I stick mostly to conversing about astronomy with old friends.

As a prospective law student, this interested has budded over time. I am most interested --for now-- in constitutional jurisprudence, administrative law, and civil rights issues. I am currently a data and analytics consultant with Kaiser Permanente.
